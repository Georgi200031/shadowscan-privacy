ShadowScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

ShadowScan is a privacy and digital exposure scanning application. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, how it is used, and how users can manage their data.

1. Information We Process

ShadowScan may process the following information when you use the app:

  • Email addresses entered for breach and exposure checks.
  • Usernames entered for public profile checks.
  • Purchase and subscription status provided by Google Play.
  • Technical request information required to operate the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed only to provide requested ShadowScan features, including Quick Scan, Deep Scan, public profile checks, purchase verification, and security-related results.

3. Third-Party Services

ShadowScan may use third-party services to provide scanning functionality, including:

  • Have I Been Pwned for breach exposure checks.
  • GitHub for public username/profile checks.
  • GitLab for public username/profile checks.
  • Google Play for billing, subscriptions, and purchase verification.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Google Play. ShadowScan does not directly collect, store, or process payment card details.

5. Data Storage and Security

ShadowScan is designed to minimize unnecessary data storage. Sensitive service credentials are kept on the backend and are not embedded inside the Android application.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained only as necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. User Control

Users can stop using ShadowScan at any time and may remove locally stored app data using the application or Android system settings.

8. Children

ShadowScan is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to independently consent to data processing.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
