ShadowScan - Data Deletion

ShadowScan provides users with an in-app option to delete identity-related data associated with the application.

How to request deletion

Delete your data directly from the ShadowScan app:
  1. Open the ShadowScan app.
  2. Open the More section.
  3. Select Delete My Identity.
  4. Review the information displayed by the app.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

What is deleted

When you use the Delete My Identity feature, ShadowScan deletes applicable identity-related data stored by ShadowScan that is associated with your use of the service.

Scan data

Information submitted for scanning may be processed only as necessary to perform the requested scan. Data processed ephemerally is not retained longer than necessary to complete the request.

Third-party data

ShadowScan may use external services to perform certain security and breach checks. Deleting data from ShadowScan does not delete information that may independently exist in third-party services, public sources, or data-breach records.

Need help?

If you are unable to use the in-app deletion option, please contact the ShadowScan developer using the contact information provided on the ShadowScan Google Play Store listing.
